### Different courses: do NOT mix these in
- **New French with Ease** (Bulger, 1998, 113 lessons) and its translations. Its TOC has e.g. "6 Les achats", and its Révision lessons have different titles and numbering. The "French With Ease series extrait" circulating on SlideShare and PDF-mirror sites is this OLD course.
- **Old Italian *Il francese* / *Il nuovo francese senza sforzo*** (Assimil Italia 2012, BnF record, ISBN 978-88-86968-32-4, 113 lezioni). Assimil.it still sells its audio as "Il Francese - Solo Mp3 Download (edizione precedente)".
- **Französisch ohne Mühe** (German base, ISBN 978-3-89625-011-7), which is still the 113-lesson course. Also the old Spanish *El francés*, ISBN 978-2-7005-0843-7, 113 lecciones.
- The **assimil.app e-course "Francese — 154 lezioni"** for Italian speakers is a different product.
- **Learn French / Salut (A2, 26 lessons)** is a different course.

## 2. How to read the table

- **Title evidence.** I could not open the extract PDFs directly: the session's network policy blocked `assimil.com`, `www.assimil.it` and `unitheque.com`. Every TOC entry below was read from the **search engine's index of the official publisher extracts**, which gave text fragments of the "Sommario/Contents" pages. Source keys:
  - **IT**: Italian official extract, `https://www.assimil.it/estratti/Assimil-Italia-Estratto-9788885695573.pdf`, mirrored at `https://monachat.assimil.online/extraits/produits/9788885695573_extrait.pdf`. Its page numbers should match the user's book.
  - **EN-g**: English 2020 extract hosted by Assimil's Japanese distributor, `https://www.gaigo.co.jp/assimil/pdf/fre_9782700508543_extrait_2020.pdf`. Its pagination equals the Italian one at every point I could check.
  - **EN-m**: English extract on Assimil's extract server, `https://monachat.assimil.online/extraits/produits/9782700508543_extrait.pdf`. Its pagination is different and about 2–30 pages lower, probably another printing.
  - **SC**: Assimil Italia SoundCloud (`https://soundcloud.com/assimil-italia`).
  - **ES**: Spanish official extract, `https://monachat.assimil.online/extraits/produits/9782700509038_extrait.pdf`.
- **Confidence**:
  - **A** = title seen in an official TOC fragment *with its lesson number printed next to it*, or in two independent extracts.
  - **B** = title seen in an official TOC fragment. The lesson number comes from the order of entries and page arithmetic, because the EN-m text layer puts the numbers one line off. Every B row fits the page sequence and the every-7th-lesson Révision pattern.
- **Italian gloss** is my own translation of the French title, not the book's Italian heading.
- **Theme/situation** and **grammar points** are **inferred** from the title and normal French-course progression unless the row says "[extract notes]". The only lessons whose notes I could see in official extracts are 1, 2, 50 and 100 (and the dialogue of 51). Nothing public lists per-lesson grammar or the sections of the Révision lessons. **Check these two columns against the book.**
- **IT p.** is the page in the Italian book where I saw it (IT or EN-g, which use the same pagination). It is a quick way to check each row against the physical Sommario.
